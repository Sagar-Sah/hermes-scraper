# Bounded live supplier pilot

These files contain real displayed Printograph calculator quotes from a run limited to one configuration per product attempt. Resumes preserve earlier samples and can add a different selection. They are not a full product catalog and do not establish independent cart verification. See `../docs/live-probe.md` for the exact validation scope and remaining gaps.

`live-pilot.csv` and `live-pilot.parquet` are downloaded from the app's completed catalog export; `live-pilot.json` retains product statuses and the selected rows. Image URLs are included. Associated image downloads remain available through the running app's export panel.


## Business Cards quantity tiers

`business-cards-tiers.csv`, `.parquet`, and `.json` contain 26 quantities from one supported Business Cards configuration. Two quantities were read directly from the calculator; 24 were calculated from captured source tables and remain verification-pending. Their production-day ranges are not inferred. The job finished in 34.03 seconds with 39 counted browser/source requests using the saved supplier session. This is not full product coverage or independent cart verification.
